From idea to cut part in five steps.

Step 1 of 5

Draw the part

Pen, rectangle, circle and hole tools snap to a 1/8 inch grid, so edges stay straight and sizes come out even. Dimensions show while you drag.

  • Hold Shift for straight and 45° lines
  • Drag a corner to reshape a part
  • Undo and redo up to 200 steps
Step 2 of 5

Line it up with VEX metal

Lay a see-through C-channel, bar, angle or plate over your sheet. Holes snap to its hole centres on the 0.5 inch pitch, so the bolts line up the first time.

Step 3 of 5

Check it before inspection

Each part is measured at its best angle and compared to the piece size limit. The cutter counts your pieces, flags sheets thicker than 0.070 inch, and warns when a hole sits so close to an edge that the plastic could crack.

If the game manual changes, you can update the limits under Rules.

Step 4 of 5

Export for your cutter or printer

SVG and DXF go straight into laser software, with kerf compensation so parts come out at the size you drew. PNG and PDF print at actual size, tiled over Letter or A4 pages if you're cutting by hand.

Step 5 of 5

Cut it and bolt it on

Your drawing saves in the browser as you work. Save gives you a project file to share with your team or open on another computer.

Pick a plastic the rules allow.

The plastic rule only allows certain non-shattering plastics up to 0.070 inch thick. Choose your sheet in the cutter and it warns you about anything that won't pass, like acrylic, which shatters.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account, no trial and no ads.

Where are my drawings saved?

In your browser, on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. Use Save to download a project file you can share or open somewhere else.

Which laser software works?

Anything that opens SVG or DXF, including LightBurn, Glowforge, xTool Creative Space and RDWorks. If a DXF comes in at the wrong size, export the DXF in millimetres instead.

What is kerf, and what should I set it to?

Kerf is the width of material the laser burns away. Set it and the cutter moves part outlines out and holes in by half that amount, so parts come out at the size you drew. 0.004 to 0.008 inch is typical; cut a test square to measure yours.

Will my part pass inspection?

The checks catch the common problems, but inspectors and the current game manual decide what's legal. Bring the manual's plastic rule to your inspection if you're unsure.

No laser cutter?

Export a PDF tiled on Letter or A4 paper, print at 100%, check the 1 inch scale bar, tape the template to your sheet and cut by hand.

Does it work on a phone or Chromebook?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser. On touch screens, drag with one finger to draw and pinch to zoom.
